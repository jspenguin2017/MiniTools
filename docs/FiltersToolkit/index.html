Domain tools return sorted, comma-separated lists and skip invalid input with warnings.

Accepts ASCII hostnames with at least two dot-separated labels, IPv4, and bracketed IPv6. Hostname labels allow letters, digits, and hyphens, but cannot start or end with a hyphen. Limits: 63 characters per label, 253 per hostname. Empty labels, underscores, trailing dots, wildcards, and negation are unsupported.

Merge and Unmerge: enter one comma-separated list of plain domains per line, without URLs, ports, or paths. Use Punycode for internationalized names. Spelling and case are preserved; matches are exact after trimming.

Links to Domains

Extract the first HTTP(S) link's domain per line, keeping duplicates. Missing or extra links produce warnings. Separate links from surrounding text with whitespace; punctuation attached to a hostname is invalid. Internationalized hostnames convert to Punycode.
